Who Pays?

Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

Who Pays? is a party game. You put your phones face down on the table and whoever picks theirs up first pays the bill. This policy explains exactly what the app records, where it goes, and how long it stays there.

The short version

There are no accounts. We never ask for your email, phone number or date of birth. We do not know who you are, we do not collect your location or contacts, and we do not sell anything about you to anyone. The app shows ads, and Google collects data for those — that part is described below.

What the app stores on your phone

This stays on your device and is never uploaded:

  • Your game history — how long each round lasted, how it ended, and the name you chose.
  • Your settings — your name and whether the siren is on.
  • A random device ID — a randomly generated identifier, created on first launch. It is not your advertising ID, not your IMEI, and is connected to nothing about you.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. There is no backup and no way for us to recover it.

What gets sent to our server

Our server is Supabase, hosted in Mumbai, India.

The name you type when joining a table is visible to the other people at that table — that is the point of it. Use whatever you like; it is not checked against anything.

Your phone's sensors and camera

  • Motion sensors — the accelerometer and gyroscope are read continuously while a round is running, because that is how the game detects a phone being picked up. These readings are processed on the phone in real time and are never stored or transmitted.
  • Camera — used only when you tap "Scan the QR" to join a table. No photo is taken, saved or sent. You can always type the code instead and never grant camera access at all.

Advertising

The app is free and paid for by a single advert shown after a round ends. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google receives data from the app in order to serve and measure those ads, typically including your device's advertising identifier, device and network information, and IP address.

How Google uses that data is governed by Google's own policies, not this one:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can limit ad personalisation on Android under Settings → Google → Ads, which lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files or messages
  • Any payment information — the app has nothing to buy
  • Your microphone

Children

Who Pays? is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. If you believe a child has used the app and you would like their data removed, contact us and we will remove it.

Your choices

  • Delete everything on your phone — uninstall the app.
  • Delete your table data — it deletes itself within a day, or leave the table and your row is removed immediately.
  • Delete your usage events — email us. Because the only identifier is a random device ID, we will need you to send it to us; it is shown at the bottom of the app's Settings screen.
  • Avoid the camera entirely — type the four-letter code instead of scanning.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what is collected, the date at the top changes and the new version appears here before the change reaches the app.
